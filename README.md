One-line elevator pitch a city pigeon gives investors for its startup:
[CooDash](https://make.fun/c/u-donrulezz-pigeon-pitch-one-line-af06c6cf) — same-day
crumb delivery, zero drivers, and every statue in the city is already a billboard.

## Entry

`main.ts` writes the pitch (169 characters, one line) to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Invest in CooDash: I deliver your fries in four minutes flat, take ten percent in crumbs, and poop on your statue for free — we're raising a seed round. Literally seeds.

Run it with Node 22+:

```sh
node main.ts
```
